The two pie charts compare the percentages of people aged 23–65 working in different occupations in Ashby and in the UK as a whole in 2008. Overall, personal services and construction were more common in Ashby than in the UK average, while professional and technical occupations were significantly less represented in Ashby.

In terms of the largest sectors, personal services accounted for 21% of workers in Ashby, compared with 17% nationally. Similarly, construction work employed 16% of people in Ashby, which was noticeably higher than the UK figure of 10%. Office work was almost identical in both locations, at 18% in Ashby and 19% across the UK.

However, there were clear differences in the professional and technical sectors. Professional work represented only 8% of Ashby's workforce, whereas the UK average was 14%. Technical work showed an even wider gap, at 9% in Ashby compared with 17% in the UK. Shop work was similar in both, at 14% and 13% respectively.
